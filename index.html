About SouthStar Lighting Solutions LLC

SouthStar Lighting Solutions LLC is a professional lighting and electrical service company built on more than 14 years of hands-on field experience, reliable workmanship, and a strong commitment to safety.

We specialize in commercial lighting and electrical solutions for retail stores, offices, showrooms, commercial spaces, and specialty environments. Our experience includes lighting repairs, LED retrofits, track lighting systems, decorative lighting installations, chandeliers, sconces, timers, lighting controls, drivers, ballasts, ceiling fans, exhaust fans, doorbells, fixture replacement, high-ceiling work, lift-based service, and electrical troubleshooting.

Our goal is to deliver clean, safe, and dependable work on every project. We understand that lighting is not only about visibility — it affects presentation, safety, customer experience, and the professional image of a business. That is why we focus on quality installation, proper troubleshooting, clear communication, and long-lasting results.

Our technical background also includes formal HVAC training, which gives us a strong understanding of electrical controls, low-voltage wiring, power requirements, equipment circuits, and breaker-tripping conditions that may be related to A/C electrical components. This knowledge helps us evaluate electrical issues safely, identify possible causes such as shorts, overloads, or control-related problems, and recommend the proper next step when a licensed HVAC contractor is required.

SouthStar Lighting Solutions LLC does not advertise or perform licensed HVAC contracting services. Our focus is lighting, electrical service, troubleshooting, and professional installation support.

We are a bilingual company. SouthStar Lighting Solutions LLC proudly serves clients in both English and Spanish, helping customers communicate service needs clearly and confidently.

We built SouthStar with a simple mission: provide honest service, fast response, safe workmanship, and quality results our clients can trust.
